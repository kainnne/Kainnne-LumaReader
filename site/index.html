WHAT IT DOES

A focused reader for the files you already own.

Point LumaReader at a folder and turn it into a calm, visual document library. Nothing needs to be imported or uploaded.

  1. 01
    Make any folder your library.

    Browse Markdown files and nested folders from one clean, collapsible sidebar.

  2. 02
    Shape the reading experience.

    Adjust text size, choose from 20 palettes, switch light or dark appearance, and read vertically, horizontally, or page by page.

  3. 03
    Render more than basic Markdown.

    Open Markdown and MDX with tables, task lists, alerts, highlighted code, KaTeX mathematics, and Mermaid diagrams.

  4. 04
    Keep media in context.

    Preview local images, audio, video, linked files, and reusable Markdown includes alongside your writing.

  5. 05
    Stay local, private, and flexible.

    Your documents remain on your computer. The app supports macOS, Windows, and an interface available in 11 languages.
