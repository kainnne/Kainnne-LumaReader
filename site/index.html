WHAT IT DOES

A calmer way to live with Markdown.

LumaReader gives an ordinary folder the feel of a considered reading app: clear hierarchy, comfortable typography, and powerful rendering without an account or a complicated setup.

  1. 01
    Focused support for Markdown and text.
    • Markdown · on by default.md, .markdown, .mkd, .mdx
    • Plain text · opt in.txt, .log
    • Format filterEnable TXT and LOG separately when needed
  2. 02
    A folder becomes a navigable reading library.

    Choose one root folder and scan its nested files with a visible elapsed-time counter. Search opens every parent folder needed to reveal a match, then restores your previous folder state when cleared; the sidebar can be widened for long names or collapsed for focus.

  3. 03
    Three reading modes, with room to breathe.

    Use a full-width vertical page, horizontal reading, or paged navigation with left/right or up/down page turns. Both axes have draggable scrollbars, and text-size or layout changes keep you close to the same reading position.

  4. 04
    Rich Markdown, without extra setup.

    Render tables, task lists, alerts, highlighted code, KaTeX mathematics, Mermaid diagrams, footnotes, emoji, abbreviations, superscript, subscript, and reusable local includes.

  5. 05
    Create, read, edit, and save in one place.

    Choose any destination folder for a new .md, confirm its name and location, and start writing immediately. Edit beside an optional live preview with a draggable split and synchronized scrolling; resize source and editor text with Command/Ctrl + or −, then save without leaving the editor.

  6. 06
    Local-first from start to finish.

    Your library stays on your computer: no account, upload, or automatic translation. LumaReader includes 11 interface languages, light and dark modes, 22 palettes with a neutral reading surface, saved preferences, and a short first-launch guide.
