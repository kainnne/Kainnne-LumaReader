WHAT IT DOES

A calmer way to live with Markdown.

LumaReader gives an ordinary folder the feel of a considered reading app: clear hierarchy, comfortable typography, and powerful rendering without an account or a complicated setup.

  1. 01
    A folder interface you already understand.

    Choose a library folder and LumaReader turns its existing structure into a clean, collapsible sidebar. Search documents, move between nested folders, and follow the outline without reorganizing your files.

  2. 02
    Designed for reading, not managing.

    The interface keeps navigation and essential controls close while giving the document visual priority. Collapse the library whenever you want fewer distractions, then return without losing your place.

  3. 03
    Make the page feel like yours.

    Adjust text size, move between light and dark appearances, choose from 20 carefully tuned palettes, and read vertically, horizontally, or one page at a time.

  4. 04
    Rich Markdown, without extra setup.

    Open Markdown, MDX, MKD, and .markdown files with tables, task lists, alerts, highlighted code, KaTeX mathematics, Mermaid diagrams, and reusable includes.

  5. 05
    Keep media inside the story.

    Preview local images, audio, video, and linked files in context, so visual references and supporting material remain part of the reading flow.

  6. 06
    Local-first from start to finish.

    Your library stays on your computer and its contents are never translated automatically. LumaReader supports macOS, Windows, and an interface available in 11 languages.
